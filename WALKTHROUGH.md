# The Keeper of Grey Harbor — Complete Walkthrough

> **Spoilers ahead.** This guide solves every puzzle and reveals the ending.
> If you're just stuck, try the in-game helpers first: the **?** button
> flashes every interactive spot in the scene, and your **Field Notes**
> (pencil button) always show your current objective.

## How the game works

- **Tap** objects, people, and doorways to look, talk, and travel.
- **Pick up** items; they go to the satchel at the bottom of the screen.
- **Use an item** by tapping it in the satchel (it lifts and glows), then
  tapping where you want to use it. Tap the item again to put it away.
- Progress **saves automatically**. You can leave any time via
  Menu → *Save & Exit to Title* and resume with *Continue*.

## Act 1 — The Dock

You arrive at Grey Harbor at dusk. Your uncle Alvar, the lighthouse keeper,
hasn't been seen in three days — but his light still turns every night.

1. **Talk to Marta**, the fisherwoman with the lantern. Ask her everything:
   - *"When did you last see my uncle?"* — Alvar stormed down the dock three
     days ago saying he'd "found the rot in the timbers of this town."
     Marta also tells you he kept a **spare key where the marigolds grow**.
   - *"Anything strange in the harbor lately?"* (appears after the question
     above) — Harbormaster **Voss** sails out on moonless nights with no
     lamps and comes back riding low. Fishing boats don't do that.
   - *"Who keeps the light burning now?"* — the light runs, but some nights
     it **dies for ten or twenty minutes**, then returns.
2. Look at the **crates** stamped "GH" near the lamppost — they're nailed
   shut. Remember them; you'll be back with a pry bar.
3. Take the **path to the lighthouse** (by the signpost).

## Act 2 — The Point

The lighthouse and Alvar's cottage stand on the headland.

1. Tilt the **marigold flowerpot** by the cottage door → **small iron key**.
2. Search the **woodpile** on the left → **rusty pry bar**.
3. Use the **iron key on the cottage door** and enter.

*(The lighthouse door is locked and needs a proper brass key. The cliff
path on the right is a sheer black drop — you can't go down yet.)*

## Act 3 — The Keeper's Cottage

Everything you need for the drawer code is in this room.

1. Tap the **fireplace** → a half-burned note: *"...the clock lies — but
   twice a day it tells the truth. Feed its numbers to the drawer."*
2. Tap the **wall clock** — it stopped long ago at exactly **7:25**.
3. Take the **oil can** from the shelf on the right.
4. Tap the **locked drawer** on the desk and enter the code:

   > **0725**  *(the stopped clock's time — a stopped clock is right twice a day)*

5. The drawer holds the **brass lighthouse key** and a **cipher wheel**
   scratched with *"seven turns for seven seas."*
6. Leave by the door (tap the rug).

## Act 4 — The Lamp Room

1. Use the **brass key on the lighthouse door** and climb the 118 steps.
2. Tap the **logbook** — the last pages are in cipher.
3. Use the **cipher wheel on the logbook**. Turn the wheel to
   **VII (seven turns)** — the entry resolves to:

   > *"Cargo moves when my light sleeps. Voss swore I would join the
   > wrecks. If I am gone, look where the light dies."*

4. Tap the **telescope** — a lamp-less launch is creeping toward the black
   stretch of cliffs no beam ever touches. "Where the light dies."
5. Tap the **great lens** to open the alignment puzzle: tap each of the
   three brass rings to rotate it 45° at a time until **all three teal
   marks point straight up**. The beam surges out — and where it rakes the
   cliffs, a carved stair and an iron gate glint in the dark.

## Act 5 — Crank and Cliffs

1. Go back down and **return to the dock**.
2. Use the **pry bar on the crates** → a **winch crank**, and the crates'
   "GH" brands are over-stamped with a crude **V**. Voss again.
3. Return to the Point and take the **cliff path** (lit by your beam now).
4. At the cave mouth, a timber gate is held by a **rusted winch**:
   - Use the **oil can on the winch** (crank alone won't turn rusted gears).
   - Use the **crank on the winch** — the gate grinds open.
5. Duck through into the sea cave.

## Act 6 — The Sea Cave

1. Examine the **stacked crates** → a smuggler's tally; every date matches
   a night the light "failed".
2. Tap the **figure by the lantern** — it's **Alvar**, alive, in hiding
   since Voss threatened him ("keepers fall from towers all the time").
   He's been copying Voss's ledger by lantern light.
3. Voss appears at the gate. When the choice comes, either option wins —
   the trap is already sprung, because **you re-lit the lens**:
   - *"Look behind you, Harbormaster."* — you tell him the cliff is lit.
   - *Say nothing. Let the light speak.* — the beam catches his loaded
     launch mid-sweep, in view of the constable's watch-post.
4. Voss runs for a tide that has already turned. Enjoy the epilogue.

| Puzzle | Answer |
|---|---|

| Great lens | Rotate all three rings until every mark points **up** |
