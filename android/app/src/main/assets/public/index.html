How to Play

  • Tap anything that looks interesting — people, doors, objects.
  • Pick up items; they appear in your satchel at the bottom.
  • Use an item by tapping it in the satchel, then tapping where you want to use it.
  • Stuck? Tap ? to reveal every interactive spot, and check your Field Notes for the current objective.
